# SOURCE MATERIALS

You have three layers of content that stack on top of each other:

## Layer 1: Curse of Strahd (Official Module)
The base adventure. Everything starts here. When expansions don't address a topic, fall back to this.

## Layer 2: Fleshing Out Curse of Strahd (MandyMod / FOCoS)
- **Source**: MandyMod's DMs Guild series + Reddit posts
- **Available Chapters**: Player Primer, Prepping the Adventure (Ch.1), Death House (Ch.2), Village of Barovia (Ch.3), Vistani & Tarokka (Ch.4), Old Bonegrinder (Ch.5), Vallaki (Ch.6), Van Richten's Tower (Ch.7/11), Krezk (Ch.8), Berez (Ch.10), Wizard of Wines (Ch.12), Amber Temple (Ch.13), Yester Hill (Ch.14), Werewolf Den (Ch.15), Castle Ravenloft (Ch.4/final), plus Extras (Vasili/Ireena/Shrine of White Sun)
- **Philosophy**: Adds depth to NPCs, fills plot holes, streamlines confusing plotlines. Adds the **Fanes of Barovia** as a major overarching quest. Makes Strahd truly immortal until the Fanes are restored. Overwrites some official content.
- **Understanding Strahd**: Deep psychological guide for roleplaying the villain.

## Layer 3: CoS Companion (Wyatt Trull)
- **Source**: Wyatt Trull's DMs Guild supplement
- **Available Content**: Complete companion matching every chapter of CoS, plus "The Wedding at Ravenloft" as Act IV finale
- **Philosophy**: Provides session-by-session advice, dialogue suggestions, stat blocks, and a structured 4-act narrative. Adds the Wedding at Ravenloft as the campaign's climactic finale. Focuses on making Strahd a "heard but not seen" menace.

## Layer 4: The Interactive Tome of Strahd
A handout-quality version of the Tome of Strahd artifact for player use.

# PRE-CAMPAIGN PREPARATION

## Session 0 Checklist
Both FOCoS and the Companion agree on these essentials:

- [ ] **Content warnings**: CoS contains child abuse, substance abuse, cannibalism, non-consensual relationships, suicide themes. Define player limits BEFORE Session 1.
- [ ] **Party size**: 3-5 players recommended (both sources agree).
- [ ] **No evil PCs**: Both sources strongly recommend against evil alignments. The campaign relies on players' good nature to progress.
- [ ] **Distribute Player Primers**: Use FOCoS Player Primer documents (Chapter 0 folder). These include Barovian character options and "Mysterious Secrets" for backstory hooks.
- [ ] **Rig the Tarokka Reading**: Both sources STRONGLY recommend pre-selecting the card reading results. (See Tarokka section below.)
- [ ] **Decide on Strahd's portrayal**: Is he a distant, enigmatic figure (Companion approach) or a more present, involved villain (FOCoS approach)?
- [ ] **Remove or nerf *Remove Curse***: FOCoS recommends requiring *Greater Restoration* instead, to make curses meaningful.

## Player Character Backstory Hooks (FOCoS)
Connect PCs to Barovia using these options:

| Hook | Description | Connects To |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Tatyana's Reincarnation** | Replace Ireena with a PC as Tatyana reborn. The PC starts unaware of past lives. | Strahd's obsession, Krezk, the entire main plot |
| **Izek's Sibling** | A PC is Izek Strazni's long-lost sibling. Izek has prophetic dreams about them. | Vallaki, Izek's doll collection |
| **Tracking Van Richten** | A PC is a former pupil/associate of Van Richten, hunting him to Barovia. | Van Richten's Tower, Vallaki (Rictavio) |
| **Freeing a Patron** | A sealed Dark Power in the Amber Temple contacted a PC, drawing them to Barovia. | Amber Temple, personal corruption arc |
| **Honoring a Holy Calling** | A divine PC receives visions of a fallen angel (the Abbot) that needs purging. | Abbey of St. Markovia, Krezk |
| **A Strange Elf** | A dusk elf PC, the last female survivor, seeking their people's redemption. | Dusk elf camp, Kasimir, Amber Temple |
| **Draconic Bloodline** | A PC with blood connection to Argynvost, the silver dragon. | Argynvostholt, the beacon |
| **Restoring the Fanes** | A Lady of the Fanes secretly befriended a PC before the campaign. Jeny Greenteeth (the Weaver) is the most likely candidate. | Fanes questline, endgame |

# KEY RULE CHANGES

## Both Sources Agree On:
- **Increase Barovia's scale**: 1 hex = 1 mile minimum (FOCoS) or 1 hex = 4 miles (Companion). Travel should take time. Use the travel time table from FOCoS Ch.1.
- **Increase populations**: Village of Barovia ~500, Vallaki ~1500-3000, Krezk ~75-150.
- **No evil PCs**.
- **Stack the Tarokka deck**.
- **Theater of the Mind** for most exploration; maps only for combat.
- **Don't show NPC portraits** to players (maintains mystery).
- **Don't show the world map** to players (maintains disorientation).

## FOCoS-Specific Rules:
- **Strahd is truly immortal** until the Fanes are restored. His Misty Escape is replaced: at 0 HP he becomes mist, immune to everything, and returns to his coffin. He resurrects at full HP the next dusk.
- **Lycanthropy overhaul**: Inherited (blessing, controllable) vs. Infected (curse, uncontrollable). Infected shift every night except new moon. *Remove Curse* doesn't work; requires *Greater Restoration*.
- **The Fanes of Barovia** are added as a major questline (see dedicated section).
- **Mother Night is replaced** by the Ladies Three throughout.

## Companion-Specific Rules:
- **The Voldemort Rule**: Speaking Strahd's name is forbidden in Barovian society. Saying it thrice is considered an invitation/summons.
- **Barovian Daylight variant**: Barovia's weak sunlight DOES harm vampires (contrary to RAW). This makes daytime feel safe and night terrifying.
- **Fear the Dark variant**: Non-Barovian darkvision doesn't work until acclimated (1d3+1 weeks).
- **Soulless Barovians**: Reverse the ratio - only 1 in 10 are soulless (not 9 in 10).
- **Heart of Sorrow**: The third stolen winery gem IS the Heart of Sorrow.
- **Argynvost the Animated**: Strahd animated Argynvost's wingless corpse. Slaying the animated corpse produces a luminous gem (the Heart of Argynvost) that replaces transporting the skull.

## RECOMMENDED COMBINED RULES:
Use FOCoS rules as the mechanical base, with these Companion additions:
1. **Use the Voldemort Rule** (great for atmosphere).
2. **Use Barovian Daylight** (makes day/night cycle meaningful).
3. **Use FOCoS Lycanthropy** (more detailed and interesting).
4. **Use FOCoS Fanes** (the major addition that makes the campaign cohere).
5. **Use the Companion's Wedding at Ravenloft** as the Act IV finale (the best endgame either source offers).

# UNDERSTANDING STRAHD

## Psychological Profile (from "Understanding Strahd" document)

**Strahd is NOT a psychopath/sociopath.** He only meets 1 of 7 criteria for Antisocial Personality Disorder (lack of remorse/rationalizing). He is:

- **Lawful**: Has a strong internal moral code. Punishes based on perceived justice.
- **Calculating**: Never impulsive. Plans everything, even murder.
- **Proud**: His deadliest sin. Believes he is always right, always desirable, always justified.
- **Delusional**: Self-justifies every horrible action. Believes his charm magic reveals "true feelings." Cannot see that he values power over love.
- **Not a liar**: Prefers half-truths and omissions. Vasili von Holtz is the exception, and even then he's "just Strahd in a fake mustache."

## How to Portray Strahd

### FOCoS Approach:
- Strahd is a tyrant who has confused love for ownership
- He prioritizes wooing Tatyana's reincarnation to turn her into a vampire spawn
- He is arrogant but frighteningly intelligent
- He won't fall for player goading or paltry tricks
- **Show, don't tell** - let players discover his nature through his contradictions

### Companion Approach:
- **Seldom show Strahd directly**. He should be heard, not seen. His presence should be felt through agents, weather, and consequences.
- Strahd appears outside Castle Ravenloft only at these key moments:
  1. The Black Carriage encounter (Area I) - delivering PCs to Ravenloft
  2. St. Andral's Feast - murders Father Lucian
  3. Abbey of St. Markovia - abducts Ireena after her resurrection/death
  4. A showdown in Vallaki - forces Van Richten to reveal himself

### Arc Words (Companion):
Seed these phrases throughout the campaign for dramatic payoff:
- **"He is the Ancient. He is the Land."** - About Strahd's connection to Barovia
- **"Be Swift. Be Smart. Beware the Night."** - Replaces "farewell" and "be safe"
- **"All Will Be Well!"** - Baron Vallakovich's delusional refrain
- **"On the Shoulder of the Shadowfell."** - The Abbot's description of Barovia's nature

### On Vasili von Holtz (FOCoS Warning):
MandyMod **strongly dislikes** the Vasili subplot for these reasons:
- Strahd is no actor; Vasili should behave identically to Strahd
- Players almost always figure it out early, making it feel cheap
- If players DON'T figure it out and Vasili romances Ireena/a PC, it creates a horrible "gotcha" that can ruin trust

**Recommendation**: If you use Vasili, keep him minimal. He's useful for the Coffin Maker's Shop setup but should not become a major recurring NPC romance.

# THE FANES OF BAROVIA (FOCoS Major Addition)

This is the single most important addition from FOCoS. It provides the overarching quest structure that the base module lacks.

## The Three Ladies

| Lady | Title | Domain | Represents | Shrine Location | Current Identity | Gift to Mortals |
|------|-------|--------|------------|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| The Seeker | Forest Fane | Forest/Ravens | The Future, Foresight | Stone circle behind Old Bonegrinder | **Madam Eva** (Eva Treenail) | Wereraven lycanthropy |
| The Weaver | Swamp Fane | Swamp/Water | The Past, Memory | Stone circle at Berez | **Jeny Greenteeth** (herbalist outside Vallaki) | Winery gemstones |
| The Huntress | Mountain Fane | Mountains/Death | Death (not evil) | Stone circle atop Yester Hill | **Lara Stoneheart** (captive of Baba Lysaga in Berez) | Werewolf lycanthropy |

## How Strahd Stole the Fanes' Power:
Shortly after becoming a vampire, Strahd desecrated each shrine:
- **Forest Fane**: Strahd captured Lugdana (a holy paladin) and turned her into vampire spawn, stealing the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind during the struggle. The corruption spread to the shrine.
- **Swamp Fane**: With Baba Lysaga's help, Strahd captured forest folk priestesses, tortured them into renouncing the Ladies, then Baba cut out their hearts and presented them to Strahd.
- **Mountain Fane**: Strahd fed undead (vampire spawn) to the Gulthias Tree. Since undead are perversions of death and enemies of the Huntress, consuming them corrupted the tree and the Fane.

## The Power Strahd Gained:
With the Fanes' power, Strahd became "The Land":
- Enhanced abilities, invulnerabilities, deeper HP well
- Literal control over nature: weather, rivers, forests, moon phases
- Control over beasts of Barovia
- TRUE immortality (not just vampire immortality)

## How Players Learn About the Fanes:

### Information Breadcrumbs (use this order):
1. **Early hints**: Small roadside shrines, the "Ring of Stone" nursery rhyme, NPCs mentioning ancient goddesses
2. **Vallaki** (mid-early): Nobility (Vargas, Fiona) know pre-Morninglord nature goddesses existed. Keepers of the Feather know the Ladies' symbolism and that Strahd stole power from them.
3. **Winery/Yester Hill** (mid): Forest folk and werewolves know the most. The three winery gems were gifts from the Ladies.
4. **Argynvostholt** (mid-late): Revenants remember Barovia before Strahd. They know shrine locations and that the Fanes can be restored.
5. **Werewolf Den** (mid-late): Werewolves know the Fanes' symbolism, shrine locations, and that restoration is possible.
6. **Amber Temple** (late): The ONLY place to learn the specific METHOD of reconsecration.

### NPC Knowledge Table:

| What They Know | Nobility | Vistani | Keepers | Van Richten/Ez | Werewolves/Folk | Abbott | Revenants | Baba Lysaga | Strahd |
|---------------|----------|---------|---------|----------------|-----------------|--------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Symbolism | No | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Shrine Locations | No | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Strahd's Connection | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Methods of Reconsecration | No | No | No | No | No | No | No | No | Yes |
| Amber Temple Location | No | No | No | No | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Restoring the Fanes (Late Game, Post-Amber Temple):

**CRITICAL**: All three must be restored before Strahd loses the power. Restoration is NOT incremental. Strahd won't sense it happening until all three are done. **Discretion is paramount.**

### Forest Fane (Old Bonegrinder Shrine):
- **Requirement**: Retrieve the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind and present it to the stone circle
- **Challenge**: A **Corrupted Wereraven Precursor** (CR 11) emerges from the largest menhir and must be defeated
- **Completion**: A single raven takes the Holy Symbol and flies away; the shrine is consecrated

### Swamp Fane (Berez Stone Circle):
- **Requirement**: Kill Baba Lysaga and present her corrupted heart at the shrine
- **Challenge**: The heart is sickly grey, woven with swamp reeds. The party experiences powerful visions of the past (the Flood, Marina's death, the torture of the priestesses)
- **Completion**: Baba Lysaga's form appears in the vision and is destroyed; the Weaver appears and takes a bite from her restored heart

### Mountain Fane (Gulthias Tree, Yester Hill):
- **Requirement**: Enter the caverns beneath the Gulthias Tree (only accessible to those who know to look)
- **Challenge**: The Trial of the Huntress - a journey through the land of the dead, reconciling with lost loved ones, confronting personal demons
- **Completion**: Reach the Huntress's altar at the cavern bottom and receive her visions

### Rewards After Restoration:
When all three are restored, the Ladies appear as Jeny, Lara, and Madam Eva, then reveal their true forms:
- **The Sunsword** (represents the Huntress - weapon to destroy foes)
- **The Orb of Rest** (represents the Weaver - manipulation of time for quick rests)
- **Proof Against Enchantment** ability for each PC (represents the Seeker - 1/long rest, auto-succeed charm save)
- **Strahd loses control** over weather, beasts, and nature. His immortality from the Fanes is stripped.

# ACT I: WELCOME TO BAROVIA (Levels 1-4)

## Death House (Level 1-3)

### FOCoS Changes:
- Split into two halves: non-hostile house exploration (upper floors) and hostile basement
- Use the Death House Script PDF for dramatic narration
- Key handouts: Mr. Durst's Suicide Letter, Mrs. Durst's Letter, Strahd's Letter to the Dursts (all in Chapter 2 folder)
- The Escaping Death House PDF covers what happens if players try to flee

### Companion Additions:
- Death House is Chapter 16 in the Companion
- Provides detailed stat blocks and encounter advice

### Key Events:
1. Party finds Death House among a cluster of buildings
2. Crying ghost children (Rose and Thorn) lure party in
3. Explore upper floors - discover family's dark history
4. Descend to basement - ritual chamber, shambling mound
5. Escape the house as it tries to kill them
6. **Level 2** at basement entry, **Level 3** on completion

### Maps Available:
None specific in your collection for Death House (use module maps).

## Village of Barovia (Level 3)

### FOCoS Changes (Chapter 3):
- Ireena is the natural-born daughter of the burgomaster (not adopted)
- If using the Tatyana-reincarnation-as-PC hook, Ireena is removed or reduced
- Meet Ismark at the Blood of the Vine Tavern - he needs help burying his father
- Mad Mary and Gertruda subplot
- Father Donavich and the trapped vampire spawn Doru in the church basement

### Companion Changes (Chapter 3):
- The funeral is a MAJOR event. A mob forms, wanting to give Ireena to Strahd
- During the funeral, an agent of Strahd arrives to demand Kolyan's corpse
- If the party triumphs, they earn the town's grudging respect
- Ismark tasks them with escorting Ireena to Vallaki or the Abbey

### Combined Event Sequence:
1. Arrive at Village of Barovia, enter Blood of the Vine Tavern
2. Meet Ismark Kolyanovich - learn about Strahd, the burgomaster's death, Ireena
3. Visit the Burgomaster's mansion - meet Ireena (bitten twice)
4. **The Funeral** (next morning at dawn):
   - Carry coffin to church graveyard
   - Father Donavich is a wreck (Doru is below)
   - Companion: A mob OR Strahd's agent disrupts the funeral. Battle possible.
5. **Evening of the Funeral**: Strahd himself appears briefly at the mansion
   - He addresses Tatyana's reincarnation by name ("Tatyana"), not Ireena
   - Proclaims love, hints at inevitability
   - Informs the party they are his future brides/entertainment
   - FOCoS: He is calm, polite, and terrifying
   - Companion: He is brief and cryptic
6. Ismark begs the party to take Ireena to safety (Vallaki or Abbey of St. Markovia)
7. Party departs for Tser Pool

### Encounter: Father Donavich & Doru
- Doru is a vampire spawn trapped in the church undercroft
- Donavich is in despair, praying ceaselessly
- The party can destroy Doru (enraging Donavich), try to help, or leave him
- **Lore drop**: Learn about the failed peasant revolt led by a "foreign wizard" (the Mad Mage of Mount Baratok)

## Tser Pool Encampment & Tarokka Reading (Level 3-4)

### FOCoS (Chapter 4):
- Detailed Vistani culture and NPC personalities
- Madam Eva is secretly the Seeker of the Forest Fane (players won't know this yet)
- The reading should feel like a powerful, destined event

### Companion:
- Madam Eva is Strahd's half-sister (canonical)
- She is manipulative but genuinely wants Strahd defeated
- The Vistani warn the party about the land: "Welcome to Barovia"

### Key Events:
1. Arrive at Tser Pool, meet the Vistani camp
2. Madam Eva performs the Tarokka reading (use your pre-selected results)
3. Party learns of the three artifacts, the Destined Ally, and the location of the Final Confrontation
4. **Level 4** upon leaving the encampment
5. Travel continues toward Vallaki

## Old Bonegrinder (Level 4, FIRST PASS)

### FOCoS (Chapter 5):
- This is a WARNING encounter, not a fight (at level 4)
- The hags (Morgantha, Bella, Offalia) are CR 5 each, with Coven abilities making them effectively CR 7+
- Players SHOULD be scared off or flee
- If they fight, this is likely a TPK
- Dream pastries subplot: Morgantha sells addictive pastries made from children's bones
- The children are kept in crates upstairs

### Companion (Chapter 6):
- Similar warning approach
- Provides detailed hag tactics and stat blocks

### How to Run It:
1. On the road to Vallaki, party sees a windmill on a hill
2. They may encounter Morgantha selling dream pastries to a desperate mother
3. If they investigate the windmill, they discover the horrible truth
4. **DO NOT let them fight at this level** - have the hags demonstrate overwhelming power
5. If they try, the hags toy with them, knock them unconscious, and dump them outside
6. Plant the seed: they MUST come back later
7. The shrine behind the windmill (stone circle) is the **Forest Fane** shrine - players won't know this yet

### Maps Available:
None specific (use module maps). The stone circle behind the windmill is FOCoS-added.

# ACT II: SHADOWS GATHER (Levels 4-7)

## Vallaki - The Powder Keg (Level 4-5)

This is the LARGEST chapter of the campaign. Players will spend multiple sessions here. Both sources dedicate enormous attention to this location.

### FOCoS (Chapter 6 - 112 page PDF!):
- Massive expansion of Vallaki's politics, NPCs, and events
- Adds the Reformation Center, St. Andral's Orphanage, expanded Town Square events

### Companion (Chapter 5):
- Structured around the St. Andral's Feast as THE defining event
- Provides Fiona Wachter's takeover mechanics in detail

### Major NPCs in Vallaki:

| **Izek Strazni** | Baron's enforcer | Demon arm. Obsessed with his lost sibling. Collection of dolls. Terrifying. |

| **Father Lucian Petrovich** | Priest of St. Andral's | Faithful, brave, and doomed. Bones of St. Andral protect the church. |

| **Urwin & Danika Martikov** | Blue Water Inn owners | Secret wereravens, Keepers of the Feather |

### The Vallaki Event Sequence:

1. Enter through gates; Izek or guards inspect the party

## Restoring the Fanes (FOCoS, Level 11)

This is the FOCoS-specific endgame buildup. After the Amber Temple, players know HOW to restore the Fanes and must visit three locations:

Once the Fanes are restored, Strahd is **enraged beyond reason**:
- He loses control over weather, beasts, and nature
- His immortality from the Fanes is stripped (he still has vampire immortality + Vampyr)
- He begins destroying the party's allies and strongholds
- It's a race to Castle Ravenloft

## The Final Confrontation

### Combined Approach:

**Before the Fight (FOCoS):**
- Players must have restored the Fanes (stripping Strahd's land-power)
- They should possess the Sunsword, Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, and Proof Against Enchantment
- They should know the ritual to summon and seal Vampyr (learned at the Amber Temple)

**The Fight Itself:**
- Location determined by Tarokka (recommended: Strahd's Tomb, K86)
- Strahd fights with everything he has - he is cornered and knows it
- His vampire brides, Rahadin, and any surviving minions join
- The Heart of Sorrow provides extra HP (if not already destroyed)
- Strahd phases through walls, uses hit-and-run tactics, and regenerates 14% HP/turn
- The Final Confrontation location is the ONLY place Strahd won't retreat from (his hubris)

**After Defeating Strahd (FOCoS):**
1. Strahd falls to 0 HP and becomes mist
2. Players must use the Amber Temple ritual to summon Vampyr
3. They must fight or seal Vampyr to break Strahd's connection
4. With Vampyr sealed, Strahd's immortality is fully gone
5. He dies permanently
6. The clouds part. The sun shines on Barovia for the first time in centuries.
7. The mists thin. Barovia is free.

**If Using Wedding at Ravenloft (Companion):**
- The confrontation happens during/after the wedding ceremony
- Strahd betrays the wedding guests, intending to make Ireena his first meal as a married vampire
- The party fights through Castle Ravenloft to reach the scene
- The drama of the wedding setting amplifies the final battle
